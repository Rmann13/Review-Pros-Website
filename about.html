Our Story

From a small idea to a simple fix

Every business owner knows the feeling: a customer says "that was great" on their way out the door, and that praise never makes it online. Review Pros exists to close that gap.

01

The problem

Asking customers to "leave us a review" and hoping they remember to search for your business later almost never works.

02

The idea

An NFC card, programmed once, that opens your exact Google review page the instant someone taps their phone to it.

03

The result

A simple, one-time-cost tool that businesses put on the counter and forget about — while the reviews keep coming in.
